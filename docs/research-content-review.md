# Research topics and preview review

Reviewed on 17 November 2026. This is a content classification review, not a peer review or replication of the results.

## Review scope

Every one of the 21 entries was checked against its primary material. For papers, the review focused on abstracts, research questions, methods, results, and conclusions relevant to classification. For presentation-only entries, the corresponding slides or poster were reviewed. The colorectal-cancer paper was initially available only through its institutional abstract and coauthor listing. The owner subsequently supplied the 15-slide GCAT 2022 presentation, which was reviewed for the workflow, experimental setup, and results. The paper's full text was not accessible.

## Grouping principles

Use substantive research questions instead of assigning every empirical paper to a generic Evaluation category. Allow overlap when a paper directly covers both themes. Keep federated learning and usable privacy visible instead of folding them into one broad privacy bucket. Do not tag agent debugging as AI control or multi-agent safety solely because it uses multiple agents.
